# Participant guide

1. **Sign in** to the CTF site with your normal account. **Competitions** in
   the top bar lists every event you can join (`/arena`).
2. **Join an event.** Open it and click **Register**. Private events need an
   invitation code: use **Join with a code** (`/arena/join`). Some events
   need organizer approval, which the page shows.
3. **Teams** (team events). On the **Team** tab, create a team (you become its
   captain and get a join code to share) or join one with a code. You can only
   be in one team per event. Captains can invite, remove members and hand
   over the captaincy. An organizer may lock teams once the event starts.
4. **Challenges.** The **Challenges** tab lists the challenges by category.
   Click one to read it, download files, unlock hints (some cost points) and
   submit flags. Wrong answers are rate-limited per team or user, and some
   challenges have a maximum number of attempts.
5. **Container challenges.** Click **Start instance** to get your own private
   copy with a host and port. It stops automatically after its time limit;
   you can restart or stop it yourself.
6. **External labs.** The **Labs** tab starts a lab session and shows its
   connection details. Only you can see those credentials.
7. **Attack & Defense** (A&D events). The **A&D** tab shows your services,
   round status, uptime, and the form for submitting captured flags.
8. **Scoreboard and progress.** **Scoreboard** shows the rankings (it may be
   frozen near the end). **My progress** shows your solves and score history.
9. **Certificates** (if the organizers enabled them) appear under **My
   progress** after the event. Each one has a public verification link and
   a code.

Your activity in an event (sign-ins, views, submissions) is logged without
IP addresses, browser details or submitted flag text, and is deleted after
the retention period the organizers set.
